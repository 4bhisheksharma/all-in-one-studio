# 🎨 Imago — All-in-One Image Workspace

**Imago** is a unified, privacy-first web application designed for organizing, editing, and transforming images all in one place.

Instead of jumping between disconnected single-purpose websites (one for compressing, one for resizing, one for cropping, and another for making collages), Imago brings your entire image workflow into a single creative workspace.

---

## 🌟 The Core Principle

> **"Upload once. Organize once. Process anywhere. Reuse everywhere."**

When you add images to Imago, they become part of your personal workspace library. You can organize them into projects and folders, open them in any tool without re-uploading, and save processed results as new assets to continue working with them anywhere in the app.

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do With Imago

### 🗂️ 1. Organize in Projects & Folders
- Upload photos and graphics directly from your device.
- Group images into dedicated projects (e.g., *Website Launch*, *Social Media Q4*, *Client Assets*).
- Create sub-folders, add tags, mark favorites, and search by filename or dimensions.
- Safely delete items to a Trash folder and restore them whenever needed.

### 🗜️ 2. Compress & Optimize
- Reduce image file sizes significantly while preserving visual quality.
- Choose target quality or aim for a specific target file size.
- Compare original and compressed images side-by-side before saving.

### 📐 3. Resize with Precision
- Scale images to exact pixel widths and heights.
- Lock or unlock aspect ratios with automatic dimension recalculation.
- Choose from ready-to-use presets for Instagram, YouTube thumbnails, Twitter headers, banners, and avatars.

### ✂️ 4. Crop, Rotate & Flip
- Freely crop images or snap to standard aspect ratios (1:1 square, 16:9 widescreen, 4:3, 9:16 story).
- Rotate by 90-degree increments or adjust angles with a precision slider.
- Flip images horizontally and vertically.

### 🖼️ 5. Build Collages
- Pick multiple images from your library and arrange them into dynamic grid layouts.
- Adjust spacing, margins, corner roundness, and background colors.
- Reposition, zoom, and swap images between collage slots.

### 🪄 6. Remove Backgrounds
- Automatically detect the main subject and erase backgrounds to create transparent PNGs.
- Replace removed backgrounds with solid colors or another image from your library.

### 👁️ 7. Blur Faces & Sensitive Details
- Protect privacy by drawing blur or pixelation boxes over faces, license plates, documents, or private data.
- Adjust blur strength per region with full undo and reset controls.

### 🔄 8. Convert Formats & Batch Process
- Convert single images or entire batches between modern formats (WebP, PNG, JPEG).
- Download converted collections individually or packaged together as a single `.zip` file.

### 🧰 9. Creative Utilities
- Inspect and strip hidden camera/GPS EXIF metadata for privacy.
- Sample colors directly from photos using an eyedropper tool.
- Automatically generate dominant color palettes.
- Add text watermarks and logos.
- Generate complete favicon and app icon packages in standard sizes.

## 🔄 Seamless Multi-Tool Workflow

Whenever you process an image in any tool, you are never locked into just downloading it:

- **Save to Project:** Add the processed result straight into your current project.
- **Save to Library:** Keep it as a fresh reusable asset linked to the original.
- **Chain to Another Tool:** Take a cropped image directly into the compressor, or take a background-removed image straight into a collage.
- **Autosaved Drafts:** Step away or switch tabs without fear—your editing progress is automatically saved so you can resume right where you left off.

---

## 🛡️ 100% Private & On-Device

Your images are yours alone:

- **No Cloud Uploads:** All processing happens entirely inside your web browser.
- **No External Servers:** Your files are never sent to a remote server or third-party service.
- **Local Persistence:** Everything is saved securely inside your browser's local database.
